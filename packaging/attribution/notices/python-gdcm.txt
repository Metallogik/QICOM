python-gdcm (https://github.com/tfmoraes/python-gdcm) packages GDCM, Grassroots
DICOM (https://github.com/malaterre/GDCM), as prebuilt native libraries. QICOM
uses it only as a pydicom pixel-data decoder, for the lossless-JPEG and JPEG-LS
transfer syntaxes that pydicom and Pillow cannot decode on their own. QICOM does
not modify it and links to it dynamically: the libraries ship as separate files
inside the bundle.

The python-gdcm wheel carries a single licence file, the Apache-2.0 text of its
own packaging code. It does not carry the licences of GDCM or of the libraries
GDCM compiles in, although its binaries contain them. Those texts are reproduced
below, verbatim (trailing whitespace removed) from GDCM v3.2.6 (commit 9c71b163416130ffdec26534444e9b85fcf0682a) - the revision
python-gdcm 3.2.6 builds, through its gdcm_src submodule - and, for OpenSSL,
from the OpenSSL repository at tag OpenSSL_1_1_1k.

Which wheel bundles what:

  all platforms   GDCM, IJG libjpeg (8, 12 and 16-bit builds, with the DCMTK
                  and lossless-JPEG patches), CharLS, OpenJPEG 2.3, Expat,
                  zlib, libuuid, md5, socket++
  Linux           also OpenSSL 1.1.1k (Red Hat Enterprise Linux 8 build,
                  python_gdcm.libs/), under the OpenSSL and SSLeay licences in
                  section 12
  macOS           also OpenSSL 3.5.2 (_gdcm/.dylibs/), under Apache-2.0: the
                  same text as section 1
  Windows         no OpenSSL; the wheel's own copy of the Microsoft Visual C++
                  runtime (bin/) is not collected into the QICOM bundle

As the Independent JPEG Group's licence requires: this software is based in part
on the work of the Independent JPEG Group.
