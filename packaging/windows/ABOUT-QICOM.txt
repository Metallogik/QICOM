About QICOM
===========

QICOM - Quality Image Comparator - is a desktop application for comparing
medical images and measuring how their quality differs. It is open-source
software (Apache License 2.0), developed for research and quality control.


What QICOM does
---------------

* Quantitative analysis. Load a reference image and one or more images to
  compare: DICOM series, NIfTI volumes or common image formats. QICOM computes
  objective image-quality metrics - full-reference metrics such as PSNR and
  SSIM, no-reference metrics such as NIQE and BRISQUE - on the whole image or
  on the regions of interest (ROIs) you draw. Heatmaps show where the images
  differ, and the results are exported to Excel together with charts and
  images.

* Qualitative evaluation. Create structured, blinded image evaluations, have
  readers complete them, and aggregate their answers.

* Radiomics. Run paired ROI/VOI feature extraction on 2D and 3D images and
  analyse how reproducible the features are.

* Utility tools. Segmentation, masks, format conversion, resampling, DICOM
  header editing and comparison, DICOM organisation and decompression, and
  anonymisation.


What QICOM is for
-----------------

Comparing two or more versions of the same image in a reproducible way: for
example different reconstruction or denoising methods, a lower dose or a
different acquisition protocol, or the opinions of several readers in a
blinded study.

Your images stay on your computer: QICOM does not send them anywhere.


Intended use
------------

For research and quality-control use only. QICOM is not a medical device and
is not intended for clinical diagnosis or for any decision concerning a
patient's care.


Citing QICOM
------------

If you use QICOM in published work, please cite:
Mattiussi, F., & Ghotra, S. (2026). QICOM: Open-Source Software for
Reproducible Image Quality Evaluation, Blinded Assessment, and Radiomic
