QICOM - Quality Image Comparator
================================

To install QICOM, double-click QICOM-Setup.exe and follow the setup wizard.
It installs QICOM in Program Files by default (you can choose another
folder), adds it to the Start menu and, if you wish, to the desktop. To
remove it later, double-click "Uninstall QICOM" in the installation folder,
or use Settings > Apps > Installed apps > QICOM > Uninstall. Your projects
and preferences are kept.

QICOM compares medical images and measures how their quality differs:
objective quality metrics on whole images and regions of interest, heatmaps
of the differences, blinded reader evaluations, radiomics and DICOM utility
tools. For research and quality-control use only - not intended for
clinical diagnosis.

Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" (SmartScreen) the first time,
because the setup is not yet digitally signed: choose "More info" and then
"Run anyway" only if your organisation allows it.
